## Keeping busy

- **I am working with Flow Money Management** as a Product Owner. Flow is a Dutch startup, [acquired](https://www.withflow.com/press-release-flow-snelstart "Read the press release about the SnelStart-Flow acquisition") by software company SnelStart. We develop embedded finance products for the B2B2X market.
- **I am guiding my team in exploring AI-native tooling** for our platform. An exciting process in which we are learning to balance using our own brains with using AI.
- **We are renovating our new home in Den Dolder**. In the meantime we live temporarily nearby, and we hope to move in early 2027.
- **I am experimenting with AI** in my spare time: a web app concept for house hunting and renovating, and keeping this now page up to date with Claude.
- **My social life is on the back burner**. With lots of change at work and a busy renovation, there is little time left for friends. A pity at times, but necessary for now.
